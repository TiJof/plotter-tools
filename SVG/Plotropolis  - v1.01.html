Plotropolis

v1.01

An isometric city generator for pen plotters

Plotropolis is a generative tool for making isometric towns and cities. It draws entirely in single-stroke line work, with SVG outputs ready to plot. Hidden lines are removed, tone comes from hatching instead of fills, and every color is its own SVG layer, so each pass of the plotter is one pen.

Who it's for

Anyone with a pen plotter! AxiDraw, iDraw, NextDraw, or anything else that takes SVG (generally with Inkscape). It is tuned for ballpoints and fineliners, and is happy with paint markers too (just watch out for long travels and remember to shake your paint!)

How to use it

  1. Pick paper and pens. Under Paper and pens, set the sheet size, your pen colors, and which pen draws roofs, shadows, water and neon.
  2. Shape the city. Every slider redraws the page. Hit Randomize for a fresh roll, or 12 variations for a sheet of “seeds” to pick from. The same seed always draws the same city.
  3. Check the numbers. The panel under the preview shows ink length, pen lifts and an estimated plot time for each pen.
  4. Save and plot. Save SVG writes one file with a layer per pen; Save one SVG per pen writes separate files. Plot the layers in the order listed, swapping pens between passes.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and your settings stay on your device.
